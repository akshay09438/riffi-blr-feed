## Drift log

*When the code and a document disagree, record it here with the date and which one was fixed.*

- 4 Oct 2026 - `docs/technical-spec.md` planned one module per route (`rss.py`, `telegram.py`, `youtube.py`, `xbackup.py`). All of them are "GET a feed and parse it", so they became one `fetchers/feeds.py` (no near-copies); the page monitor stays its own module. Spec updated.
- 4 Oct 2026 - Google News resolution caps online look-ups at 100 per run (the brief gives no number; the panel project only said "cap it"). Revisit after the first live run on the laptop.
- 4 Oct 2026 - The brief's `token_set_ratio >= 85` alone merges different stories: it scores 100 whenever one title's words are inside another ("Metro fare hike" / "... rolled back"), and 86-98 for titles that differ only in a number, party, team or place. Added two rules (no conflicting detail; shorter title covers >= 70% of the longer's words) and anchored the 48 h window on a story's first report, chosen on labelled pairs in `tests/test_dedupe.py`.
- 4 Oct 2026 - The brief says page monitors ignore "date-stamp-only changes". Removing every date before comparing would also hide real changes (a new effective date), so only stamp *lines* are ignored (e.g. "Last updated: 04/10/2026 10:32 AM", visitor counters); dates inside content still count.
- 5 Oct 2026 - The brief's step 7 speeds (Google News on High-priority topics every 30 min) and APScheduler gave way to D-008: Windows Task Scheduler and `fetch --due`, Google News every 2 h during the test. The log is `engine.log` beside the database (like `fetch.lock`), not a separate logs folder, so test databases never write into the real log. Spec and plan updated.
- 5 Oct 2026 - The page monitor's "trafilatura main text" (spec, step 1) was blind on real data: S004 DIPR, S109 BMTC, S110 BWSSB and S048 Kannada & Culture all stored the same 3,588-character Kannada privacy policy from the karnataka.gov.in template, and S009 GBA and S008 ECI stored "You need to enable JavaScript to run this app.". The page is now pruned of policy blocks and `<noscript>` before extraction, a page with no text left is a "needs JavaScript" error, and a blind old snapshot is re-baselined without an item. Built against a fixture modelled on the template (`tests/fixtures/karnataka_gov_template.html`); the real pages still need checking on the laptop (`test-feeds`). Spec updated.
- 5 Oct 2026 - The first design of the scheduler counted a `blocked` error as a Google refusal. In the code that kind only means a link to X, Instagram or WhatsApp was refused, so a Google refusal is a Google News feed answering 403 or 429. The design (`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-05-scheduler-design.md`) was rewritten to as-built after the safety review, which also added: a `failed` diary row for a check that cannot start, a strict closing of diary rows, `status` telling a running fetch from a dead one by trying the run lock, and a due rule that ignores fetch stamps dated in the future.
- 5 Oct 2026 - Before the diary table was added, the database was backed up (`data/engine-backup-2026-10-05.db`, gitignored; counts and integrity checked). `tests/conftest.py` now points every test at a temporary database, so a test that forgets `--db` can never write into `data/engine.db`.
- 5 Oct 2026 - The digest's 24-hour window is chosen on `items.fetched_at` (a story is in it when any of its reports was fetched in it), not on `story_clusters.updated_at` as `stories --hours` does: `fetched_at` is indexed and records when the engine actually saw the report, while `updated_at` is rewritten whenever a story grows. The brief's fixed 07:00 IST digest became "the 24 hours before the command is run", with `--date` for the 24 h ending 07:00 IST on a past day (D-012), because there is no timer (D-009). Spec updated.
- 5 Oct 2026 - `feeds.csv` notes said PIB's RSS works with `Regid=1` and that `ViewRss.aspx` lists a Bengaluru feed. In reality PIB keeps the language and region in a browser session: without one, Bengaluru (`Regid=20`) is empty and Delhi (`Regid=3`) is in Hindi with no dates, and the release listings need JavaScript. S011 and S107 became Google News searches of `site:pib.gov.in`; `feeds.csv`, the brief's route counts and `tests/test_inputs.py` were updated together.
- 5 Oct 2026 - The brief's 7-day filter assumes feed dates are honest. Google News re-dates old Cricbuzz scorecard / squads / commentary pages and IPL highlight videos (seen on S035's backup query q=RCB: about half of the digest's top 30), so cleaning now also drops cricket match-record pages by title pattern (a match named "A vs B" plus a page-name suffix, or "Highlights: ... | TATA IPL"). A season year alone is not a drop rule, so news that mentions a past season is kept. Spec updated.
- 5 Oct 2026 - A live run split one story ("Namma Metro timings extended on October 3") into three clusters: "October 3" vs "3 October", "extended" vs "extends", and "in Bengaluru" read as a conflicting detail. De-duplication now compares titles in a matching form (one date format, stemming, no stop-words, Bangalore = Bengaluru, a Namma Metro/BBMP/BMTC... title counts as saying Bengaluru). Two of the three now join; the third ("services to run beyond midnight") is the same story in other words and stays split - joining it by title would also join a same-day Metro fare story - so it is a strict expected failure in `tests/test_dedupe.py` for the AI pass to handle. Labelled pairs: 13 same, 19 different, 2 known splits. Spec updated.
- 5 Oct 2026 (evening, laptop) - The page-monitor pruning above did not hold on the real pages. After `fetch --all` with the new code, S004 DIPR, S048 Kannada & Culture, S109 BMTC and S110 BWSSB answered 200 and still stored the same 3,588-character Kannada privacy policy (one shared hash, `eb00a106...`), so the real karnataka.gov.in markup is not what the fixture assumed. S008 ECI and S009 GBA answered 304 Not Modified to the conditional request, so the new rules never saw their pages and the stored "You need to enable JavaScript to run this app." stays: a blind snapshot has to be re-fetched without validators, the way a first visit is. The real S004 and S109 pages are now fixtures (`tests/fixtures/karnataka_gov_real_*.html`). On them, every template policy modal has a heading the title list does not know (plural, other words, other spellings), and the only changing content, the "latest news" list, is itself in a hidden modal (`#newsModal`), so the fix must keep that one and drop the rest. Details are in `docs/zuko-handoff.md`. The technical spec notes the gap.
